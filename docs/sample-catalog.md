## Recipe assumptions

Recipes serve two people: 180 g of dried pasta or 250 g of filled pasta. Filled pasta uses vegetarian cheese or ricotta/spinach fillings. Use vegetarian hard cheese for the pesto. Cook pasta to package instructions and reserve a mug of pasta water. Sauce preparation happens alongside pasta cooking; times are estimates. Salt is included in every recipe.

## Sauces

### Tomato & basil

A comforting tomato sauce with fresh basil and a little garlic.

- Category: Tomato base
- Estimated total time: 20 minutes
- Tags: Vegetarian
- Sauce ingredients for two people: 400 g Chopped tomatoes; 1 clove Garlic; 6 leaves Basil; 1 tbsp Olive oil

1. Warm the oil over medium-low heat. Add chopped garlic and stir for 30 seconds without browning it.
2. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Simmer gently for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Tear in the basil. Add the cooked pasta and a splash of reserved pasta water, then toss gently until coated.

### Spicy tomato

Tomato, garlic, and chili: a bold sauce with an adjustable kick.

- Category: Tomato base
- Estimated total time: 20 minutes
- Tags: Vegetarian, Spicy
- Sauce ingredients for two people: 400 g Chopped tomatoes; 1 clove Garlic; ¼ tsp Chili flakes; 1 tbsp Olive oil

1. Warm the oil over medium-low heat. Add chopped garlic and chili flakes; stir for 30 seconds.
2. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Simmer for 15 minutes. Taste and add more chili if you like.
3. Add the cooked pasta with a splash of pasta water and toss until coated evenly.

### Creamy mushroom

Golden mushrooms and a silky cream sauce for a cozy, easy meal.

- Category: Cream & cheese
- Estimated total time: 25 minutes
- Tags: Vegetarian
- Sauce ingredients for two people: 200 g Mushrooms; 100 ml Cream; 1 clove Garlic; 1 tbsp Olive oil

1. Slice the mushrooms. Heat the oil over medium heat and cook the mushrooms for 8–10 minutes until golden.
2. Add chopped garlic and cook for 30 seconds. Stir in the cream, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 3 minutes.
3. Add the cooked pasta. Loosen with reserved pasta water as needed and toss gently before serving.

### Lemon & ricotta

A soft, creamy sauce brightened with lemon. Mix it off the heat.

- Category: Cream & cheese
- Estimated total time: 15 minutes
- Tags: Vegetarian
- Sauce ingredients for two people: 150 g Ricotta; ½ lemon Lemon; 1 tbsp Olive oil; To taste Black pepper

1. In a large bowl, mix ricotta, oil, finely grated lemon zest, and 1 teaspoon of lemon juice.
2. Stir in a few tablespoons of hot pasta water until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Add the cooked pasta and toss gently off the heat. Add more pasta water if the sauce is too thick.

### Basil pesto

Fresh basil, nuts, and olive oil make a fragrant sauce with no cooking needed.

- Category: Fresh & cold
- Estimated total time: 15 minutes
- Tags: Vegetarian
- Sauce ingredients for two people: 30 g Basil; 20 g Pine nuts; 30 g Vegetarian hard cheese; 3 tbsp Olive oil; ½ clove Garlic

1. Blend basil, pine nuts, garlic, grated vegetarian hard cheese, and oil into a paste.
2. Put the pesto in a large bowl and loosen with 2 tablespoons of warm pasta water. Taste and season with salt.
3. Toss with the cooked pasta off the heat. Add more pasta water if needed; do not boil the pesto.

### Fresh tomato & mozzarella

Cherry tomatoes, mozzarella, and basil for a fresh, warm-weather bowl.

- Category: Fresh & cold
- Estimated total time: 15 minutes
- Tags: Vegetarian
- Sauce ingredients for two people: 200 g Cherry tomatoes; 125 g Mozzarella; 6 leaves Basil; 1 tbsp Olive oil

1. Halve the tomatoes and dice the mozzarella. Combine with torn basil and oil in a large bowl; season with salt and pepper.
2. Drain the pasta and spread on a plate for a few minutes until warm rather than steaming hot.
3. Gently toss the pasta with the tomato mixture and serve. The sauce stays fresh and uncooked.
